# Better Pets (NeoForge)

Owning a dog, cat, parrot or horse, done right. `/pets` lists every pet you
own and where it is, even in unloaded chunks. Find it with a glow and an
on-screen arrow, call it to you, rename it (colors and gradients
included), give it to a friend.

- Your own hits don't hurt your pets; leads on your pets never snap
- Death alert with coordinates; server side, works with vanilla clients

Requires **Mycel**. Needs NeoForge, Minecraft 26.3, 26.2 or 26.1.2, Java 25.
