# Better Pets

Owning a dog, cat, parrot or horse, done right. `/pets` lists every pet you
own and where it is, even in unloaded chunks. Find it with a glow and an
on-screen arrow, call it to you, rename it, give it to a friend. Server
side: players need nothing installed.

- `/pets`: all your pets with health, distance, direction and buttons
- `/pets find`: the pet glows, an arrow above your hotbar points the way
- `/pets call`: teleports a pet to you
- `/pets name`: rename without a name tag, with `&` colors or MiniMessage gradients
- `/pets give`: hand a pet to another player
- Your own hits don't hurt your pets (sneak to hit anyway)
- Leads on your pets never snap, the pet is pulled along
- Death alert with the exact coordinates
- Config: `enabled`, `protectFromOwner`, `strongLeads`, `deathAlerts`,
  `allowCall`, `callCooldownSeconds`, `allowRename`, `allowGive`, `findSeconds`
- Requires **Mycel**: install it alongside this mod

Reload live with `/mycel reload betterpets`.
